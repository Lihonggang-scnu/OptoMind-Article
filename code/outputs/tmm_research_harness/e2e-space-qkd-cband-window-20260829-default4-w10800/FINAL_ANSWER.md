# TMM research and design result

## Problem interpretation

I am responsible for the R&D of the protective window for the front-end of a satellite-based quantum key distribution receiver. The receiver needs to receive 1530-1565 nm telecom band single-photon signals as completely as possible, while reflecting 400-700 nm visible light solar background as much as possible to reduce the impact of space solar scattering and detector stray light on quantum signal reception. Please design a planar multilayer dielectric film using only HfO2 and SiO2 under conditions of unspecified material incidence and fused silica substrate, with a total stack layer count not exceeding 30 layers.

## Method research

- No reusable literature method was available; planning used explicit optical theory assumptions.
