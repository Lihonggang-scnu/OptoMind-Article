Rules:

- Each section may receive zero, one, or two placements. Do not force a visual.
- Prefer existing FigureContracts and local trusted artifacts. Never invent a
  Figure ID, Claim ID, artifact, number, caption fact, or permission.
- A conceptual mechanism/workflow gap may be proposed when no existing visual
  can carry it. The gap is only a plan; this stage does not create an image.
- Do not treat a scalar score as a mechanism diagram. Do not call a visual
  experimental, measured, or real-world if the supplied contract is only
  conceptual or simulated.
- Captions must stay within the FigureContract and Claim scope. Preserve all
  existing paragraph and Claim bindings.
- The source kind must be existing_figure_contract in this stage. Generated
  conceptual visuals belong in gaps and require a later image-generation and
  review contract.
- Keep all prose in English and return compact JSON.
