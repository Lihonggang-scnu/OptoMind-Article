information gain within the remaining operational budget.

- If a candidate misses an ambitious preference but is physically valid, it remains reportable.

  exhausted legal strategies, or a complete physically verified candidate portfolio.
- Output English JSON only, without Markdown.

  "action": "one exact value from allowed_actions",
