# TMM research and design result

## Problem interpretation

Design a planar multilayer dielectric coating for a multispectral imaging camera using TiO2 and SiO2 on a fused silica substrate. The design requires high reflectance in the 450-650 nm band and high transmittance in the 700-1000 nm band at 45 degrees incidence, with a maximum of 24 layers.

## Method research

- **Quarter-Wave Baseline Initialization**: Start optimization from a quarter-wave stack structure (HL)^N at a design wavelength lambda0 before refining to non-quarter-wave thicknesses to meet complex spectral targets.
- **Needle Optimization Technique**: Use needle optimization algorithms to iteratively insert or adjust thin layers within the stack to minimize a merit function for multi-wavelength targets.
- **Tolerance Analysis for Robustness**: Simulate layer thickness and index errors (e.g., 2% RMS) during the design phase to verify performance robustness before fabrication.
- **Protective Outer Layer Termination**: Terminate the stack with a thick low-index layer (e.g., SiO2 >= 200 nm or approximately half-wave) to improve environmental durability and manage standing wave field intensities.
- **Global Optimization with Bounded Thickness**: Explore configuration space using a global optimization strategy where each layer's physical thickness is bounded (e.g., 0 to lambda0/4) to find robust solutions.
